## Context

Jio Points are rural and urban retail stores and Jio Centers are regional offices. They needed a simple way to run daily store operations.

## What I built

- A retail store **super app**: JP Works for Jio Points and JioWorks for Jio Centers, optimized for low-spec devices
- **Lean management** techniques applied to store operations
- Onboarding of 1,100+ stores in rural India and a complete offline business for Reliance Digital
- The go-to-market strategy and super app for the **Jio Prime Merchant POS**

## Results

- 83% less operational activity time for Jio Points
- 3-day turnaround from purchase to delivery pan India
