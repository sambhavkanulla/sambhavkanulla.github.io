## The challenge

Order-driver assignment decides how fast and how cheaply a delivery happens. I owned and revamped the driver search and allocation algorithm for a food delivery service.

## What I did

- A redesigned **driver allocation algorithm** that uses resources freed up from early deliveries
- **Driver behavioral features** that shorten pickup and drop-off

## The outcome

- Delayed tail-end deliveries down 16%
- Average order pickup and drop-off time down 5 minutes
