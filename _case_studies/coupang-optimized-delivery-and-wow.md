## Context

I owned the order-driver assignment algorithm, the search and allocation of drivers, along with the delivery products for 200k+ drivers. Separately, Coupang's WOW loyalty program has a 14M+ user base, and bringing it into Coupang Eats was a major growth lever.

## What I owned

- **Assignment product:** revamped the driver search and allocation algorithm for speed and cost
- **Optimized Delivery:** ideated, designed and implemented the product
- **WOW launch:** strategised and launched the MVP of Eats' flagship Free Delivery service for WOW members

## Results

- Reduced delayed tail-end deliveries by 16% by using resources freed from early deliveries
- Improved average order pickup and drop-off time by 5 minutes with driver behavioural features
- Optimized Delivery improved average delivery time by 12 minutes (7 minutes during bad weather and driver shortages) and reduced delayed orders by 9%
- It was later re-skinned as Coupang Eats' flagship Free Delivery for WOW, with 70%+ customer opt-in
- Eats sales grew 7x in 3 quarters after the WOW launch
