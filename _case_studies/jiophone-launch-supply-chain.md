## Context

JioPhone was India's first 4G-supported feature phone, built for rural India, and one of the most awaited launches in the country. It started as a voucher- and invite-based launch, with several variants made by multiple vendors with different features.

## What I owned

- A voucher-based interest and invite aggregation, with order-variant-inventory allocation and supply chain for 6M+ JioPhones, from procurement to supply to sale to activation, pan India
- Voucher-based order management for 6M+ vouchers and leads, with a pull strategy by pin code
- A FIFO-based aggregation tool for variant management

## Beyond the launch

- **FTTx (fibre to the home):** work-order flows, service-engineer issuance and inventory capitalisation, and supply chain and payment processes for 50k+ devices with 100% serial-number visibility
- **Store super apps:** JP Works for Jio Points and JioWorks for Jio Centers, optimised for low-spec devices, cutting operational activity time by 83% at Jio Points
- **Onboarding:** automated authorization and onboarding for 1M+ supply-chain users, with access assigned in under 5 hours
